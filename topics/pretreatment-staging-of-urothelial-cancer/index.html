Pretreatment Staging of Urothelial Cancer: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

3 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Pretreatment Staging of Urothelial Cancer", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Bladder cancer, muscle invasive, pretreatment staging

Adult. Muscle invasive bladder cancer. Pretreatment staging.

May be appropriate: US pelvis (bladder); MRI abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; MRI pelvis without IV contrast; Bone scan whole body; CT chest without IV contrast; FDG-PET/MRI skull base to mid-thigh; CT abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast
