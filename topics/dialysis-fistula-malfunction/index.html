Dialysis Fistula Malfunction: which imaging to order

38 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Dialysis Fistula Malfunction", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.
