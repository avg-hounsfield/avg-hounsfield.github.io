Vomiting in Infants: which imaging to order

10 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Vomiting in Infants", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Usually Appropriate Fluoroscopy upper GI series

4 scenarios

Vomiting, bilious, few distended bowel loops on radiography, next imaging study

Bilious vomiting within the first 2 days after birth. Radiographs show a nonclassic double bubble with gas in the distal small bowel, or few distended bowel loops, or a normal bowel gas pattern. Next imaging study.

May be appropriate: US abdomen (UGI tract)

ACR source for this scenario
