Brain Tumors: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

7 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Brain Tumors", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Brain tumor metastatic, screening, extracranial malignancy

Adult. Secondary or metastatic brain tumor screening. Extracranial malignancy.

May be appropriate: MRI complete spine without and with IV contrast; MRI head without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario

Brain tumor on prior imaging, extra-axial suspected, pretreatment evaluation

Adult. Suspected extra-axial brain tumor on prior imaging. Pretreatment evaluation.

May be appropriate: MRI complete spine without and with IV contrast; MRI functional (fMRI) head without IV contrast; MRI head without IV contrast; DOTATATE PET/CT brain; DOTATATE PET/MRI brain

ACR source for this scenario

Brain tumor primary, screening, genetic risk factors

Adult. Primary brain tumor screening. Genetic risk factors.

May be appropriate: MRI complete spine without and with IV contrast; MRI head without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario
