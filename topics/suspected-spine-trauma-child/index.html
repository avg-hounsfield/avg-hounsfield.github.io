Suspected Spine Trauma-Child: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

9 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Suspected Spine Trauma-Child", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Usually Appropriate Radiography cervical spine

7 scenarios

Neck trauma, GCS < 14 Pediatric

Child, younger than 3 years of age, acute cervical spine trauma, Pieretti-Vanmarcke weighted score greater than or equal to 2 to 8 points. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: MRI cervical spine without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario

Neck trauma, abnormal mental status or neuro exam Pediatric

Child, 3 to 16 years of age, acute cervical spine trauma, at least one risk factor with reliable clinical examination (based on PECARN or NEXUS). Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: MRI cervical spine without IV contrast; CT cervical spine without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario
