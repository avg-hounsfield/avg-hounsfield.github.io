Imaging of the Axilla: which imaging to order

12 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Imaging of the Axilla", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Breast cancer, >2cm, mid treatment neoadjuvant chemotherapy, initial clinical node negative, new palpable axillary lump, axilla imaging

Female. Breast cancer, greater than 2 cm in size, at mid-treatment of neoadjuvant chemotherapy, with initial clinical node-negative disease but now presenting with new palpable axillary lump. Imaging of the axilla at mid-treatment.
