Pulmonary Arteriovenous Malformation (PAVM): ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

11 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Pulmonary Arteriovenous Malformation (PAVM)", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

PAVM suspected, TIA, lung nodule on chest radiography, next imaging study

Adult. Presenting with a transient ischemic attack, or seizures, or brain abscess, or altered sensorium. Chest radiography reveals a lung nodule. Suspected pulmonary arteriovenous malformation (PAVM). Next imaging study.

May be appropriate: US echocardiography transesophageal with IV contrast; Arteriography pulmonary; MRA chest without and with IV contrast; CT chest with IV contrast; CT chest without and with IV contrast; CTA chest with IV contrast
