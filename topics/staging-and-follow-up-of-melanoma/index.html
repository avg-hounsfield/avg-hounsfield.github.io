Staging and Follow-up of Melanoma: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

157 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Staging and Follow-up of Melanoma", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Cutaneous melanoma, abdomen, newly diagnosed, microscopic satellite on primary lesion biopsy, initial staging

Adult. Newly diagnosed cutaneous or muco-cutaneous melanoma. Microscopic satellite in primary lesion biopsy specimen, or positive lymph nodes on wide excision or sentinel lymph node biopsy, or suspected regional lymph node involvement based on signs or symptoms. Initial staging.

May be appropriate: US abdomen; US axilla; US chest; US groin; MRI abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; MRI abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; MRI head without and with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast; CT chest with IV contrast
