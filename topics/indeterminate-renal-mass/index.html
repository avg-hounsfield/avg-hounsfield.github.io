Indeterminate Renal Mass: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

3 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Indeterminate Renal Mass", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Usually Appropriate US abdomen with IV contrast; US kidneys retroperitoneal; MRI abdomen without IV contrast

1 scenario

Renal mass, indeterminate, contraindication to iodinated and gadolinium based contrast, initial imaging

Indeterminate renal mass. Contraindication to both iodinated CT and gadolinium-based MR intravenous contrast. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: CT abdomen without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario

Usually Appropriate US abdomen with IV contrast; MRI abdomen without and with IV contrast

1 scenario

Renal mass, indeterminate, contraindication to iodinated contrast, initial imaging

Indeterminate renal mass. Contraindication only to iodinated CT intravenous contrast. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: US kidneys retroperitoneal; MRI abdomen without IV contrast; CT abdomen without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario

Usually Appropriate US abdomen with IV contrast; MRI abdomen without and with IV contrast; CT abdomen without and with IV contrast

1 scenario

Renal mass, indeterminate, no contraindication to iodinated or gadolinium based contrast, initial imaging

Indeterminate renal mass. No contraindication to either iodinated CT contrast or gadolinium-based MR intravenous contrast. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: US kidneys retroperitoneal; MRI abdomen without IV contrast; CT abdomen with IV contrast; CT abdomen without IV contrast; CTU without and with IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario
