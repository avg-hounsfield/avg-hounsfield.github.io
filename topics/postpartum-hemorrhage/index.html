Postpartum Hemorrhage: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

4 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Postpartum Hemorrhage", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Postpartum hemorrhage, <= 24 hrs after cesarean delivery, initial Imaging

Postpartum hemorrhage. Early (within first 24 hours) after cesarean delivery. Initial Imaging.

May be appropriate: CT abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; CTA abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario

Postpartum hemorrhage, >24hrs to <= 6wks hrs after cesarean delivery, initial Imaging
