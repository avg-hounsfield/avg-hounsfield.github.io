Chronic Hip Pain: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

22 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Chronic Hip Pain", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.
