Chest pain, high prob acute coronary syndrome, initial imaging

Chest pain, high probability for acute coronary syndrome. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: US echocardiography transthoracic resting; US echocardiography transthoracic stress; MRI heart function and morphology without and with IV contrast; CTA coronary arteries with IV contrast; SPECT or SPECT/CT MPI rest only; SPECT or SPECT/CT MPI rest and stress

ACR source for this scenario

Chest pain, low to intermed prob acute coronary syndrome, initial imaging

Chest pain, low to intermediate probability for acute coronary syndrome. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: US echocardiography transthoracic resting; MRI heart function and morphology without and with IV contrast; MRI heart with function and inotropic stress without and with IV contrast; MRI heart with function and inotropic stress without IV contrast; MRI heart with function and vasodilator stress perfusion without and with IV contrast; CT coronary calcium; CTA chest with IV contrast; SPECT or SPECT/CT MPI rest only; Rb-82 PET/CT MPI rest and stress
