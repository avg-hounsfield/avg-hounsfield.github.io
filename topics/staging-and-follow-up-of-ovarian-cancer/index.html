Staging and Follow-up of Ovarian Cancer: which imaging to order

5 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Staging and Follow-up of Ovarian Cancer", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.
