Lung Cancer Screening: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

14 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Lung Cancer Screening", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Usually Appropriate CT chest without IV contrast screening

2 scenarios

Lung cancer screening, >=20 pack yrs smoking history, quit in past 15 yrs, initial imaging

Lung cancer screening. Patient 50 to 80 years of age and 20 or more packs per year smoking history and currently smoke or have quit within the past 15 years. Initial imaging.

ACR source for this scenario
