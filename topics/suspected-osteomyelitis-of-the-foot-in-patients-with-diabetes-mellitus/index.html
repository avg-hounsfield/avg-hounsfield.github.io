Suspected Osteomyelitis of the Foot in Patients with Diabetes Mellitus: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

7 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Suspected Osteomyelitis of the Foot in Patients with Diabetes Mellitus", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.
