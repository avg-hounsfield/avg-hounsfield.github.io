Which imaging to order

20 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Pelvic Floor Dysfunction in Females", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.
