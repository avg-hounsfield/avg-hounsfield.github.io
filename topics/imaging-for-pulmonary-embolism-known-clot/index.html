Imaging for Pulmonary Embolism, Known Clot: which imaging to order

3 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Imaging for Pulmonary Embolism, Known Clot", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Recurrent embolic disease suspected, hx of acute pulmonary embolism, initial imaging

Adult. Known history of acute pulmonary embolism. Suspected recurrent or residual embolic disease. Initial imaging.

May be appropriate: US echocardiography transthoracic resting; MRA chest without and with IV contrast; MRA chest without IV contrast

Thromboembolic disease, chronic, surveillance

Adult. Known chronic thromboembolic disease. Surveillance.

May be appropriate: US echocardiography transthoracic resting; Arteriography pulmonary; Arteriography pulmonary with right heart catheterization; MRA chest with IV contrast; MRA chest without and with IV contrast; MRI heart function and morphology without and with IV contrast; MRI heart function and morphology without IV contrast; V/Q scan lung; V/Q scan with SPECT or SPECT/CT lung
