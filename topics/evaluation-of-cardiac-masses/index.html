Which imaging to order

Cardiac mass on echocardiography, unknown etiology, next imaging study

Adult. Known cardiac mass in echocardiography. Unknown etiology. Next imaging study.

May be appropriate: MRI heart function and morphology without IV contrast; DOTATATE PET/CT skull base to mid-thigh; MIBG scan whole body with SPECT or SPECT/CT chest; Octreotide scan with SPECT or SPECT/CT chest and abdomen

ACR source for this scenario
