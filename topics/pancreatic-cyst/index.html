Pancreatic Cyst: which imaging to order

5 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Pancreatic Cyst", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Pancreas cyst, <=2.5cm, incidental, initial evaluation

Incidentally detected pancreatic cyst less than or equal to 2.5 cm in size. Initial evaluation.

May be appropriate: MRI abdomen without IV contrast with MRCP; CT abdomen with IV contrast multiphase

ACR source for this scenario

Pancreas cyst, >2.5cm, incidental, no high risk features, initial evaluation

Incidentally detected pancreatic cyst greater than 2.5 cm in size. No high-risk stigmata or worrisome features. Initial evaluation.

May be appropriate: US abdomen endoscopic; MRI abdomen without IV contrast with MRCP; CT abdomen with IV contrast multiphase

ACR source for this scenario
