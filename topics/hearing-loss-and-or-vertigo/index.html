Hearing Loss and/or Vertigo: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

8 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Hearing Loss and/or Vertigo", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.
