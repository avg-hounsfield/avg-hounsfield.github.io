Staging and Surveillance of Testicular Cancer: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

10 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Staging and Surveillance of Testicular Cancer", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Usually Appropriate Radiography chest; MRI abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast; CT chest with IV contrast

5 scenarios

Testicular cancer, nonseminoma, post orchiectomy, initial staging

Initial staging of nonseminoma testicular cancer. Diagnosed by orchiectomy.

May be appropriate: MRI abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; MRI head without and with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; CT chest without IV contrast

ACR source for this scenario

Testicular cancer, stage 1A, nonseminoma, recurrence suspected, post orchiectomy, surveillance

Surveillance of stage IA and IB pure seminoma and nonseminoma testicular cancer. Diagnosed by orchiectomy. Suspected recurrence.

May be appropriate: US scrotum; MRI abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; MRI head without and with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; CT chest without IV contrast; FDG-PET/CT whole body

ACR source for this scenario
