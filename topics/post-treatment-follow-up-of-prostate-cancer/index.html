Post-Treatment Follow-up of Prostate Cancer: which imaging to order

7 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Post-Treatment Follow-up of Prostate Cancer", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Prostate cancer, recurrent disease suspected, post nonsurgical local and pelvic treatment, follow-up imaging

Prostate cancer follow-up. Clinical concern for residual or recurrent disease after nonsurgical local and pelvic treatments.

May be appropriate: MRI-targeted biopsy prostate; TRUS-guided biopsy prostate; MRI abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; MRI pelvis without IV contrast; Bone scan whole body; Choline PET/MRI skull base to mid-thigh; CT abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast; Fluoride PET/CT skull base to mid-thigh
