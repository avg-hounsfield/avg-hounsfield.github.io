Screening, Locoregional Assessment, and Surveillance of Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

8 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Screening, Locoregional Assessment, and Surveillance of Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, locoregional staging

Adult. Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Locoregional disease staging or pretreatment planning or posttreatment evaluation related to neoadjuvant therapy or surgical planning.

May be appropriate: MRI abdomen without and with hepatobiliary contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; FDG-PET/CT skull base to mid-thigh
