Staging and Follow-up of Pancreatic Neuroendocrine Tumors: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

6 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Staging and Follow-up of Pancreatic Neuroendocrine Tumors", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor, non-liver dominant disease, post treatment follow-up imaging

Adult. Pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor. Follow-up imaging after treatment. Non-liver dominant disease.

May be appropriate: MRI abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; MRI abdomen without and with IV contrast with MRCP; MRI abdomen without IV contrast with MRCP; CT abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; CT chest abdomen pelvis without and with IV contrast; FDG-PET/CT skull base to mid-thigh
