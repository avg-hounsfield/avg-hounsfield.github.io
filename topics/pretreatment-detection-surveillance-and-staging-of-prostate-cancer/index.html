Which imaging to order

6 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Pretreatment Detection, Surveillance, and Staging of Prostate Cancer", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.
