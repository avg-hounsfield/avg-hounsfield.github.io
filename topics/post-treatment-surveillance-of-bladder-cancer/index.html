Post-Treatment Surveillance of Bladder Cancer: which imaging to order

5 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Post-Treatment Surveillance of Bladder Cancer", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.
