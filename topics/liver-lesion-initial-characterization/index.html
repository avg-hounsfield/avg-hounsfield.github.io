Liver Lesion-Initial Characterization: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

15 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Liver Lesion-Initial Characterization", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Independent summary of the ACR Appropriateness Criteria as of November 2025. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the American College of Radiology. For education; confirm against the linked source and your institution's protocols.

Liver lesion, >1cm, indeterminate on US, hx of extrahepatic malignancy

Indeterminate, greater than 1 cm liver lesion on initial imaging with US. Known history of an extrahepatic malignancy.

May be appropriate: US abdomen with IV contrast; Image-guided biopsy liver; MRI abdomen without IV contrast; DOTATATE PET/CT skull base to mid-thigh; CT abdomen without and with IV contrast; FDG-PET/CT skull base to mid-thigh; Octreotide scan with SPECT or SPECT/CT chest and abdomen
