Recurrent Lower Urinary Tract Infections in Females: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

5 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Recurrent Lower Urinary Tract Infections in Females", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Usually Appropriate MRU without and with IV contrast; CTU without and with IV contrast

4 scenarios

UTI, lower urinary tract, recurrent, complicated

Recurrent lower urinary tract infections in a female. Complicated, or patients who are nonresponders to conventional therapy, develop frequent reinfections or relapses, or have known underlying risk factors.

May be appropriate: US kidneys and bladder retroperitoneal; Fluoroscopy voiding cystourethrography; Fluoroscopy cystography; MRI abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; MRI abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis without IV contrast; CT abdomen and pelvis without and with IV contrast; CT pelvis with bladder contrast (CT cystography)
