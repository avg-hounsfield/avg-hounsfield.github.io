13 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Head Trauma-Child", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Head trauma, chronic, blunt, new or progressive neurologic deficit, not abuse or post traumatic seizure Pediatric

Child. Chronic blunt head trauma with new or progressive cognitive or neurologic deficits. Excluding suspected abusive head trauma and post-traumatic seizure.

May be appropriate: MRA head without IV contrast; MRI head without and with IV contrast; MRI head without IV contrast with DTI; CT head without IV contrast
