Imaging of Suspected Intracranial Hypotension: ACR Appropriateness Criteria summary

Which imaging to order

9 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Imaging of Suspected Intracranial Hypotension", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Headache, chronic, daily, intracranial hypotension suspected, initial imaging negative, hx and clinical exam suggesting CSF leakage, next imaging study

Adult. Chronic daily headache from suspected intracranial hypotension with negative initial brain and spine imaging, but with history and clinical examination suggesting CSF leakage. Next imaging study.

May be appropriate: MR myelography complete spine; DTPA cisternography
