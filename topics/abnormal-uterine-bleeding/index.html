Which imaging to order

4 clinical scenarios from the ACR Appropriateness Criteria topic "Abnormal Uterine Bleeding", grouped by the imaging study ACR rates highest for each. Studies listed together share the same ACR category; ACR does not rank studies within a category.

Uterine bleeding, abnormal, US inconclusive, follow-up imaging

Abnormal uterine bleeding. Follow-up imaging when original ultrasound is inconclusive or further imaging characterization is needed.

May be appropriate: US duplex Doppler pelvis; US pelvis transabdominal; US pelvis transvaginal; MRI pelvis without IV contrast
