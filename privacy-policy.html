1. About GRAi Imaging Guide

GRAi Imaging Guide is an educational platform designed to provide imaging protocol information and guidelines for healthcare professionals. Our mission is to serve as a reference tool for qualified medical personnel in their professional practice.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect your information. The site has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Searches run entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent to us or to anyone else.

No patient information: We do not collect, store, or process any personal health information (PHI) or patient data. Please do not enter patient identifiers into the search.

Saved on your device: Your browser keeps a few preferences, such as your recent searches and display settings, and a copy of the site so it works offline. These stay on your device and are never transmitted to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Hosting: The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of each page request, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or use that data.
